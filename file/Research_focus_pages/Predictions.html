Physics-based prediction models

To accommodate large-scale engineering applications, we establish efficient, physics-based prediction models using aerodynamic theories to predict wake dynamics and rotor loadings, which subsequently serve as the foundation for computing both tonal and broadband noise radiation. Using these physical models, we investigate a wide range of flight states, such as hover and forward flight, while also evaluating coaxial rotor configurations, installation effects, and transitional maneuvers.

By Mr. Zimo Wang
