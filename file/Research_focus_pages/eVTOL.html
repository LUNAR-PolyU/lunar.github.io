Full-scale aeroacoustic prediction for eVTOL aircraft

We specialize in three-dimensional computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to resolve complex unsteady flow physics and noise generation around electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft during flight. Our framework captures intricate mid-scale rotor wake dynamics and aerodynamic interactions between rotors and adjacent airframe components, while quantifying and comparing the drag contributions of the rotors, fuselage, strut, and landing gear under diverse operating conditions. Furthermore, the methodology resolves both near-field aeroacoustic sources and far-field noise propagation to support low-noise airframe design and flight-envelope optimization.

By Mr. Qidong Chen
