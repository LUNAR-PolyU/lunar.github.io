Ground effect on rotor aerodynamcis and noise features

High-fidelity computational aeroacoustics studies by solving the Navier-Stokes equations are conducted to investigate the ground effects on rotor aerodynamics and noise. Durign the take-off and landing phases, it is inevtiable that the wakes shed from the rotors will impinge to the group. with high-fidelity turbulence models to capture the complex flow field, followed by acoustic analogy methods to predict far-field noise radiation. The presence of the ground surface introduces significant aerodynamic and acoustic modifications: the rotor wake causes enhanced vortex-ground interactions and generates jet flow near the ground, which not only alters thrust and power characteristics but also intensifies noise generation. Accurate CFD-based aeroacoustic prediction in ground effect is essential for assessing community noise impact and optimizing rotor design for urban air mobility scenarios.

By Mr. Qidong Chen
