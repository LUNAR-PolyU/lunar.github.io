Advanced Computational Aeroacoustics (CAA) Solver

We specialize in high-order computational aeroacoustics (CAA) to investigate complex turbulent flow physics and acoustic generation around three-dimensional boundaries. By solving the compressible Euler and Navier-Stokes equations with low-dissipation and low-dispersion numerical schemes, our solver framework accurately captures complex aeroacoustic phenomena, including resonant cavity flow noise, turbulent boundary layer interactions, and acoustic generation around airfoils and turbomachinery configurations.

By Dr. Sinforiano Cantos
