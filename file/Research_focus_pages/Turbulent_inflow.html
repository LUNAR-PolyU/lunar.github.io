Turbulent inflow interaction with rotor

Utilizing synthetic turbulence within high-fidelity simulations, we study the environmental turbulence interacting with rotors and multi-rotor flying vehicles. The incoming turbulence from the generation region interacts with the rotor wake, causing the tip vortices to lose their symmetry, deform rapidly, and break down, eventually altering the noise features.

By Ms. Yufan Bi
