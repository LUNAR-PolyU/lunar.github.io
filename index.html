About the group

Research at the intersection of fluid mechanics and sound.

Based in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, our group is dedicated to advancing the understanding and control of unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) aerodynamic noise. We develop physics-based prediction models, perform high-fidelity simulations, and conduct experiments to capture the complex flow physics and acoustic signatures of UAS propulsion systems: from conventional drone rotors to electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft and urban air mobility (UAM) platforms. In parallel, we integrate machine learning and data-driven techniques into our modelling pipeline, enabling faster and more robust predictions across broad parameter spaces.

Our goal is to translate fundamental knowledge into actionable insights for quieter, more efficient, and more sustainable urban aerial vehicles. Beyond engineering solutions, we also investigate the human perception of rotor noise through annoyance studies, bridging acoustics with community impact. Ultimately, we aim to make future urban air transport not only possible, but also socially acceptable to the communities it serves.

Unsteady Aerodynamics

Aeroacoustics

Physical mechanisms of tonal and broadband noise and advanced acoustic analogies.

03

Computational methods

High-fidelity simulation, turbulence modelling, reduced-order models, and acoustic prediction.

04

Psychoacoustics

Psychoacoustic metrics, perceptual modelling, and subjective evaluation of rotor noise.

05

AI-enhanced aeroacoustics

Machine learning for surrogate modelling, flow-acoustic source identification, and data-driven prediction.

06

Next-generation flight

Quiet propulsion and aerodynamic technologies for UAS.

GROUP LEADER

Prof. Siyang Zhong

Assistant Professor

BSc (Peking University), MEng (Peking University), PhD (HKUST), MHKIE, MRAeS

Prof. Siyang Zhong is currently an Assistant Professor at AAE, PolyU. Before joining the Department, he worked as a Research Assistant Professor at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) with a junior fellowship at HKUST Jockey Club Institute for Advanced Study. He studied at Peking University for Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Mechanics and Control Theory respectively. He received his PhD Degree in Mechanical Engineering at HKUST in 2018. He is interested in the fundamental studies of aeroacoustics, aerodynamics, and their applications to practical engineering problems. He works in several working groups to develop national and international standards for the unmanned aircraft system.
